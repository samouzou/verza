Food/Recipe · How To/Creator

Two niches. Different tension. Same job: greenlight long-form that can win.

Food / Recipe

Process tension

What usually works: visual payoff early, dish + drama in the thumbnail, series formats (“30-day,” “I tried X for a week”). What usually fails: recipe dumps with no arc.

How To / Creator

Transformation tension

What usually works: identity payoff, outcome + emotion in packaging, essay + system (Struthless model). What usually fails: tip lists with no stakes.

How we advised · Struthless
