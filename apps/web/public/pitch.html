Problem

Creators built the audiences. Platforms and brands still own the economics.

  • Creators wait 45+ days (often 60–120) to get paid for work already delivered.
  • They spend hours chasing invoices instead of creating.
  • FB, IG, YouTube keep moving the monetization goalposts — gatekeeping audiences creators built.
  • Loyal mid-size followings often beat vanity 1M accounts stuck on platform RPM rules.
  • Matching with brands that already need their niche still takes double work and cold outreach.
  • Brand campaign cycles drag — planning and fulfillment stretch while creators sit idle.
  • Global South creators stay locked into differential pricing and weaker payout rails.

Solution

Verza puts creators at the center of the money and the match.

Get paid

Contracts, invoices, wallets, Stripe Connect, and global payout paths — so settlement isn’t a side hustle.

Get matched

Campaigns with briefs and Optic discovery so brands find niche creators without months of spreadsheet casting.

Own the outcome

Per-creator links and codes, performance rewards (CPC/CPA), and a Store to monetize fans off-platform.

Stay global

Payout infrastructure built for creators outside US-only banking — including Global South settlement paths.
