Problem

Brands spend billions on creators. Most of it is still unattributed faith.

  • Flat sponsorship fees with no click or conversion truth.
  • Spreadsheets, shared codes, screenshot “proof,” and delayed invoices.
  • Affiliate networks weren’t built for creator briefs, submissions, and global payouts.
  • Ad platforms optimize for their metrics — not named creators who drive your store.

The missing piece is campaign → attribution → wallet → payout in one system.

Insight

Creators are a sales channel — treat them like one.

The winning stack looks less like a marketplace directory and more like financial rails + attribution: unique links and codes per creator, wallet-backed rewards, contracts when you need them, and payouts that clear.

Brand side

Launch hybrid or pure-performance campaigns. See per-creator clicks and store conversions. Fund rewards from a wallet.

Creator side

Join, get a unique link or code, submit work, earn performance bonuses, cash out via Connect or global payout rails.
