Brand deals are great — but you shouldn’t have to wait on a campaign to earn. Store is live inside Verza: sell to the people who already follow you, with a link you can drop in your bio, Stories, or newsletter.

You can set up:

  • Tip jars — accept one-time support from fans
  • Downloads — sell a file, pack, or resource
  • Courses — multi-lesson content with a public outline and private chapters

Fans check out once (no account required). You get paid to your connected payout account. Verza takes 10% + normal card processing — no monthly store fee.

Getting started takes a few minutes:

  1. Open Store in your Verza account
  2. Connect payouts in Settings (if you haven’t already)
  3. Create a tip jar, download, or course → set it Active
  4. Share your product link anywhere your audience already is
Open your Store

Campaigns on Verza stay exactly where they are — Store is how your community can pay you when there’s no brand brief on the calendar.
