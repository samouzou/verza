# Banned claims & sensitive patterns — Verza LinkedIn

Everyone (including agents) must pass drafts against this list before **approve** or **publish**.

## Never say (unless legal + product have signed off on exact wording)

- Guaranteed outcomes: “guaranteed viral,” “guaranteed ROI,” “you will make $X.”
- Legal/tax advice: “you don’t need a contract,” “write this off as…,” “this is tax-free.”
- Unverified numbers: competitor stats, market size, Verza revenue/users unless approved for public use.
- Misrepresenting product: features that do not exist; implying instant payouts without approval steps.

## High caution (rewrite or add disclaimer)

- **Verza Score** as a promise of performance (it is a quality signal in-product; marketing should describe it accurately).
- **Fees / splits** — only use numbers from current approved pricing/comms; when in doubt, say “see app” or “typical flow.”
- **Creator income** — do not imply creators “always” earn; focus on process fairness and clarity.

## Tone boundaries

- No punching down at creators, small agencies, or “brands are stupid” pile-ons.
- Competitors: critique **category habits**, not named companies, unless PR/legal approves.

## AI disclosure (pragmatic)

- If a post is **mostly** agent-drafted and asserts product behavior, add a light human edit in voice; optional short note in internal calendar that draft was AI-assisted (for your own QA).
